# Meeting tasks

Turn what was said in a meeting into decisions on the record and tasks in the task manager.

## When to use
After a meeting: "pull the action items from this," "what did we decide and who owns
what," or when a transcript lands from the meeting recorder.

## Workflow
1. **Read the transcript.** From the connected meeting recorder, or one they paste or point
   you to.
2. **Extract three things:**
   - **Decisions** made, one line each.
   - **Action items:** the task, the owner, and a due date if one was said.
   - **Open questions** left unresolved.
3. **Confirm the owner and date** where the meeting left them implicit. Ask, do not assume.
4. **Add the action items to the task manager** when it is connected, or hand back a clean
   list to drop in.
5. **Summarize** the meeting in a few lines for the record.

## Keep it honest
- Only capture what was actually said. Flag anything ambiguous rather than inventing an
  owner or a date.
- Nothing is sent or assigned outside the firm without approval.
