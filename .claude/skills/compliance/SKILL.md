# Compliance and risk check

A first-pass read for the exposures that matter to this business. It is a screen, and it
does not replace counsel or an auditor.

## When to use
"Check this contract for risk," "are we handling [data] correctly," "what are the
obligations in this policy," "flag anything that could bite us."

## Tune it to their field
Ask, or infer from the client file, what regime applies, then check against it:
- **Law firm:** conflicts, client confidentiality, trust / IOLTA handling, filing and
  response deadlines, engagement terms.
- **Healthcare:** HIPAA and PHI handling, consent, retention.
- **Regulated data or finance:** access controls, retention, auditability, consumer-data
  rules.
- **Any contract:** auto-renewal, termination windows, liability and indemnity,
  exclusivity, data and IP terms.

## Workflow
1. Read the document, or inspect the process or data in question.
2. List findings by **severity**, worst first, each tied to the specific clause, field, or
   step it refers to.
3. State the obligation and the gap plainly, with a suggested fix or question.
4. End with the line that matters: "Have [counsel or the right specialist] confirm [X]
   before relying on this."

## Keep it honest
- A screen, not legal advice. Say so.
- Cite the exact source (clause, table, step). Never invent a rule. If you are unsure which
  regime applies, ask.
