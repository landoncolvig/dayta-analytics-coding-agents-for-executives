# Research

Pull together a fast, sourced briefing the executive can act on, instead of opening twenty
tabs.

## When to use
"Give me background on [company or person]," "what do we know about this opposing party or
vendor," "summarize the new [rule] and what it means for us," "size up this market."

## Workflow
1. **Pin the question.** What decision is this for? That sets how deep to go and what
   matters.
2. **Search across angles, not one query.** The web, plus anything connected (their data,
   their documents) when the question is internal.
3. **Verify before you assert.** Cross-check key claims against more than one source. Flag
   what is uncertain instead of smoothing over it.
4. **Write the briefing:** a 3-4 sentence bottom line, the handful of facts that matter, the
   risks or watch-items, and the open questions. Cite the source for anything
   consequential, with links.
5. **Offer next steps:** a deeper dive on one thread, a draft outreach (`email-reply`), or a
   one-pager (`brief` or `deck-builder`).

## Keep it honest
- Cite sources. If you cannot verify something, say so.
- Separate what is known from what is inferred.
- Prefer recent facts, and note the date of the key ones.
